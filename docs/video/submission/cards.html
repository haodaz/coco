How it's built

Four Meta models, one rule: only what happened.

Muse Spark
reads each photo, writes the card, answers as your pet with cited cards, checks weather and rules, runs the playdate
Muse Image
paints every card in a blind-box style, the 3D character and its moves, the portrait that sharpens
SAM 3.1
cuts the pet out of each painting so it moves in front of the scene, and cuts the character's frames
Muse Voice
turns what you say about a moment into text, so you can just talk
