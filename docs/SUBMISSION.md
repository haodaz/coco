## Project name

cocoverse

## One line

A little universe for you and your pet: an AI twin of your pet, built only from moments that really happened.

## Problem statement

Build for Your World.

## Description

Your pet will never tell you who they are. Pet "AI" today is either a chatbot that knows breeds, or a filter on a photo. cocoverse builds your pet's AI twin the only honest way: from what the animal actually does.

Every photo you share becomes a collectible card, a Memory Print: Muse Spark reads the moment into short notes and a line in the pet's voice; Muse Image paints a new picture of your pet in that moment, in a blind-box style; SAM 3.1 cuts the pet out so rare cards have real depth. You add the story the photo can't show, and your words outrank anything the model guessed.

The twin grows only from those cards. It answers as your pet, in your language, and names the cards each answer rests on. Asked about something it has never been shown, it says so and suggests the photo that would teach it. It never makes a memory up: an answer can only cite cards that exist, a single photo never becomes a habit, and general breed knowledge is labelled as such. Under every answer it shows what it did: how many moments it looked through, what it checked, which cards it cited.

Around it, a small world: Memory Points and daily packs; series that follow who your pet is; a portrait of the pet that sharpens from a mosaic to a painting as evidence grows; a memory graph of everything it knows, every link resting on real cards; a 3D character of your pet that walks over and reacts. Around us checks today's weather, whether a parked car is safe right now, and the local rules with their sources, so when you ask your pet along to the shop it checks first. Friends: a sitter can talk to the twin through a link, and before two pets meet, their twins meet first (agent to agent) and both owners get one honest note.

Faces are blurred on the device before anything is uploaded. No health, food-amount, medicine or training advice. Any pet.

## Meta models

- Muse Spark 1.3: identification, photo reading, the pet's answers with cited evidence, the soul portrait, the memory graph.
- Muse Image 1.0: every card painting, the pet's portrait at each clarity level, the 3D character and its moods and walk.
- SAM 3.1: the pet layer on rare cards and every character frame.
- Muse Spark Open (open weights, on Baseten): sitter answers, twin-to-twin playdates, the memory graph; the page says so when it answered.
- Muse Voice Transcribe 1.0: spoken questions and stories.

## Demo notes for judges

- Coco is a real dog; Biscuit (corgi) and Mochi (cat) are demo pets whose photos were made with Muse Image.
- A new account walks: three questions → one photo → Starter Kit → "Bring to life" (about two minutes) → talk.
- Live calls take time: a card 20–40 s, a pack opens at once from paintings made in the background.
